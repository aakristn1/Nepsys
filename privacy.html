Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Nepsys Technologies (ABN 31 515 938 374) (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, business name, phone number and email address.
  • Information about your enquiry, and anything you tell us on a call, by text, email or chat.
  • Recordings and transcripts of calls to our demo line or AI receptionist, and chat transcripts, where these features are used.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends when you visit this website, such as your IP address.

How we collect it

We collect information when you fill in a form on this website, call or text us, use our chat, book a call, or become a client. We don’t buy contact lists.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and book calls.
  • To provide, monitor and improve our services.
  • To send you information about our services, where you’ve agreed to this. You can opt out at any time.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

Marketing and the Spam Act

We only send commercial texts and emails with your consent, identify ourselves in every message, and include a way to unsubscribe. To opt out, reply STOP to a text, use the unsubscribe link in an email, or contact us.

AI assistants

Some calls and chats may be handled by an AI assistant. We aim to make that clear at the start of the conversation. If you’d prefer to speak with a person, ask, or contact us directly.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run our business, including:

  • GoHighLevel (HighLevel, Inc.), our customer relationship, messaging, booking and AI platform.
  • Web3Forms, which delivers website form submissions to us.
  • Google, which provides the fonts on this website and our email.

Some of these providers store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States. We take reasonable steps to make sure they handle it in a way consistent with the APPs. We don’t sell personal information.

Our clients’ customers

When we run a system for a client business, we handle their customers’ information on that business’s behalf and according to their instructions. If you’re a customer of one of our clients, please contact that business first about your information.

Cookies and analytics

This website doesn’t currently use analytics or advertising tracking. If chat or booking tools are enabled, those providers may set cookies needed for them to work. We’ll update this policy if that changes.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we delete or de-identify it when it’s no longer needed.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or make a privacy complaint, by contacting us. We’ll respond within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
