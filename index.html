The problem

Every call you miss while you’re busy is business that goes to whoever picks up next.

Whether they’re after an accountant, a mortgage broker or an HVAC technician, people rarely wait for a call back. They ring the next number on Google. Voicemails go unreturned, website forms land in an inbox nobody checks, and quotes go cold.

You don’t need another admin hire to fix it. You need a system that answers, captures and follows up for you, and someone to run it properly.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Something not covered here? Call 0432 457 880 or ask on the free call.

Do I need a new phone number?

Usually not. In most setups your existing number stays on your website and business cards, and unanswered calls are forwarded into the system. We’ll check what works with your phone provider on the first call.

What is GoHighLevel, and do I have to learn it?

GoHighLevel is the software platform we build your system on. It handles the texts, pipeline, bookings and follow-ups. You don’t have to learn it. We set it up and run it, and you can use the simple mobile app to see and reply to leads if you want to.

Will the Virtual Receptionist handle every call?

No, and we won’t pretend it does. It answers inbound calls, asks your qualifying questions, books appointments into your calendar and transfers urgent calls to a person. Anything outside what you’ve approved, it takes a message or passes to you. Callers are told they’re speaking with an AI assistant.

How do the Google review requests work?

After you mark the work as complete, the customer gets a text or email asking them to leave a Google review. Every customer gets the same request. We don’t filter out unhappy customers or steer anyone away from leaving a review.

Why don’t you show prices?

Every business is set up a bit differently, depending on your phone setup, website and how you take bookings. It’s a one-off setup plus a monthly service. After a 15-minute call we’ll give you a clear quote in writing.

What happens each month?

We monitor the system, fix anything that breaks, tweak messages and follow-ups when something isn’t working, and send you a monthly report on leads, bookings and missed calls.

What’s the free missed-call audit?

We look at how calls and enquiries currently reach you, and where they slip through: after-hours calls, voicemails nobody returns, website forms that go to an old inbox. You get a short summary of what we found, whether or not you work with us.
