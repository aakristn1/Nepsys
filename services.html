Services

Both are a managed service: we set everything up, then keep it running each month with monitoring, tweaks and a results report.

Offer 01

Never Miss a Lead

The core system. It catches every enquiry, gets it booked, and follows up on the ones that go quiet.

Book a free 15-minute call

  • Missed-call text-back

    When you can’t answer, the caller gets a text from your business straight away, so the conversation keeps going instead of going to a competitor. You can reply from the app on your phone.

  • Chat and form capture

    A chat widget and forms on your website feed into one simple pipeline, along with texts and calls. Every lead is in one place with its history.

  • Online booking

    Customers book a time that suits from your available slots. Bookings sync to your calendar, and reminders go out automatically to cut no-shows.

  • Follow-up on unbooked leads

    If someone enquires but doesn’t book, a short follow-up sequence checks in by text or email. We write it with you, in your voice.

  • Google review requests

    After you mark a job as complete, every customer gets the same request to leave a Google review. We don’t filter out unhappy customers or steer anyone away from reviewing.

Offer 02

Never Miss a Lead + AI Receptionist

Everything in the core system, plus an AI voice assistant that answers your inbound calls when you can’t.

Callers are speaking with an AI assistant, and we set it up to tell them so. It’s there to answer, qualify, book and escalate. It doesn’t replace your staff and it won’t handle every situation.

Book a free 15-minute callTry the demo

  • Answers inbound calls 24/7

    During jobs, after hours, on weekends. Callers get a real conversation instead of a voicemail beep.

  • Qualifies the enquiry

    Asks the questions you’d ask: what the job is, where it is, how urgent it is. Answers go straight into the lead’s record.

  • Books the job

    Offers available times and books directly into your calendar, using the same booking rules as the rest of your system.

  • Escalates urgent calls

    Calls that match your rules for “urgent” are transferred to you or your team. Anything it can’t handle, it takes a message and flags it.

  • Works inside limits you set

    It only discusses what you’ve approved, like services, service areas and hours. It doesn’t quote prices or give technical advice unless you’ve told it exactly what to say.
