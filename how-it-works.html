Onboarding steps

  1. Discovery call

    15 minutes. We learn how calls, enquiries and bookings reach you now, and where they slip through. If we’re not a fit, we’ll say so.

  2. Build

    We set up your system on GoHighLevel: text-back, pipeline, booking, follow-ups, review requests and, if you choose it, the Virtual Receptionist.

  3. Test

    We run real test calls, texts, chats and bookings end to end, and you check the messages before anything reaches a customer.

  4. Go live

    We switch it on, walk you through the mobile app, and stay close in the first weeks to fix anything that needs adjusting.

  5. Monthly reporting

    Every month we monitor, tweak and send a plain-English report on leads, missed calls caught, bookings and review requests.

What we need from you

A little access. No tech work.

We’ll guide you through each of these during setup. Most of it is a few minutes on the phone.

  • Your phone setup

    Who your phone provider is, so we can set up call forwarding for unanswered calls.

  • Your calendar

    Access to the calendar you already use, plus your working hours and the job types you take bookings for.

  • Your website

    Access to add the chat widget and forms. No website? We’ll talk through options.

  • Your Google Business Profile

    Your review link, so review requests go to the right place.

  • How you talk to customers

    Your usual questions, service areas and what counts as urgent. We turn that into the messages and the AI’s instructions.
